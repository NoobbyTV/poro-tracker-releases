Changelog

What's new in each version. The browser extension's version is in brackets. Download the latest.

0.4.0 (extension 3.2.0) — October 31, 2026

  • New: an optional Poro Tracker account. Sign in with Discord in Settings → Account, and your matches, decks and notes, and the replays you wrote notes on, are kept in your account: they follow you to other computers, and the app and the browser extension stay in step even when only one is open.
  • Replays without notes stay on your computer. Settings → Account can delete your account and everything in it at any time; what's on your computer stays.
  • In Firefox, signing in first asks your OK to send your history to your account. Turning that off in Firefox's add-on settings signs you out.
  • The browser extension is on the Chrome Web Store and Firefox Add-ons, and updates itself. The app's Settings and porotracker.com link to both.

0.3.2 (extension 3.1.1) — October 29, 2026

  • Poro Tracker has a website: porotracker.com, with downloads, features, the changelog and the privacy policy. Questions and bug reports: contact@porotracker.com.
  • Settings in the app has a link to download the browser extension, with install steps; the extension's Settings has a link to download the app. Both go to porotracker.com.
  • When the extension can't sync with the app, it now says why (the app isn't running, it's an older version, or the browser blocks it), and the app's Settings says whether it's waiting for the extension.
  • In the browser, clicking the section that's open hides the panel; the button now says so.
  • Lighter in the browser: the extension now only listens to the game's own connection, not Rift Atlas's other live data, which was read all the time while signed in.
  • New setting in the extension: turn off "Move Rift Atlas over to make room" to have the panel float over the game instead.

0.3.1 (extension 3.1.0) — October 28, 2026

  • The browser extension's panel is now part of the Rift Atlas page, like the app: the rail on the left, the panel beside the game, and the game moved over to make room instead of covered. The toolbar button and Alt+T show and hide it.
  • Smoother games in the browser, especially in Firefox: the extension does much less work on the game's own thread.

0.3.0 (extension 3.0.0) — October 28, 2026

  • New: the Poro Tracker browser extension for Chrome and Firefox (and Edge and Opera). The same tracker as the app, for games you play in the browser.
  • New: while the app is open, it and the extension share your matches, decks, notes and replays, both ways.
  • Matches saved in the old 2.x extension are brought in when it updates.
  • Deleting a match, deck or replay now asks "Sure?" right where you clicked.

0.2.2 — October 28, 2026

  • Loading decklists for an older match no longer switches the deck you're tracking.
  • Sideboarding in a Bo3 keeps the same deck instead of adding a copy, and the deck tracker counts the list as played.
  • Fixing a Bo1 result by hand also fixes going first/second, battlefield and game win rate stats.
  • Smoother while playing: the side panels don't redraw on every game update.

0.2.1 — October 28, 2026

  • Neeko decks: the legends Neeko can copy are no longer taken for the legend you play.
  • History: one compact line per match (result, the opponent's legend, score, date) that opens for details.
  • Tidier Decks and Replays, and safer saving when quitting, moving your data folder or loading a backup.

0.2.0 — October 28, 2026

  • The first public version: live score and deck tracking, match history, stats, Insights (a report on each deck, read from every game's replay), replays with coaching notes, and your game on your Discord status.
  • Updates itself on Windows (installer) and Linux (AppImage).
  • Choose where your stats and replays are saved, turn saving off, and back everything up to one file.
