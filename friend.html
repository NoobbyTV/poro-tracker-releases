Add a friend on Poro Tracker

Someone sent you their friend code for Poro Tracker, the free tracker for Riftbound on Rift Atlas. Friends can invite each other to their groups, where everyone's matches come together: a shared matchup grid, the week's leaderboard, each other's replays, posted decks, and who's playing right now.

How to add them

  1. Get Poro Tracker: the app for Windows or Linux, or the browser extension for Chrome or Firefox. Already have it? Make sure it's up to date.
  2. Open Friends, the person-with-a-plus icon in Poro Tracker's rail, and sign in with Discord.
  3. Add a friend: paste the code, or this page's link, and send the request. You're friends once they accept.

Being friends shares no matches: it lets you invite each other to groups. Your friends see your Discord name and picture. More in the privacy policy.
