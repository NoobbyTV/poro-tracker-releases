Track every Riftbound game you play.

A live deck tracker while you play, then your match history, stats, Insights on every deck, and replays with coaching notes. As an app for Windows and Linux, and right inside Rift Atlas in Chrome and Firefox.

Everything after the game, and during it

Live deck tracker

See what's left in your deck and your odds of drawing each card next, updated as you play. Cards that go back into your deck are added back.

  • Your deck is detected from Rift Atlas, even after sideboarding in a Bo3.
  • The score, the race to 8 and the games of the match at a glance.

Match history

Every match with its result, score, date and the opponent's legend. Open one for both decklists, notes and its replay.

  • Results come from Rift Atlas's own record of the game.
  • Filter by deck, and see how each deck does against each legend.

Insights for every deck

A report on each deck, read from the replay of every game: its curve and card types, how often each card is drawn, played or stuck in hand, your mulligans, and how it does against each legend, going first or second, and after sideboarding.

Stats and the matchup grid

Win rates per deck, best of 1 and best of 3, streaks, game length, battlefields, and a grid of every legend you've played against every legend you've faced.

Replays with coaching notes

Rift Atlas deletes its replays after a few hours; Poro Tracker keeps them. Watch them in Rift Atlas's own viewer, flag mistakes and good plays at the exact action, and send the replay with your notes to a student or a friend.

Right inside Rift Atlas in your browser

The browser extension puts the same rail and panel into the Rift Atlas page, with the game moved over to make room. Hide it with a click or Alt+T. While the app is open, both share your matches, decks, notes and replays.

Chrome, Edge, Opera

The browser extension, from the Chrome Web Store. Updates itself.

Add to Chrome

Edge and Opera can add it from the Chrome Web Store too.

Or install the .zip by hand
  1. Download the zip and unzip it.
  2. Open the extensions page (chrome://extensions) and turn on Developer mode.
  3. Click Load unpacked and pick the unzipped folder.
  4. Open Rift Atlas: the Poro Tracker rail appears on the left.

Firefox

The browser extension, from Firefox Add-ons. Updates itself.

Add to Firefox

Rift Atlas laggy in Firefox? In Firefox's Settings → General → Performance, untick "Use recommended performance settings", tick "Use hardware acceleration when available", and restart Firefox.
