Privacy

Last updated November 13, 2026

Poro Tracker is a free fan-made tracker for Riftbound on Rift Atlas: a desktop app for Windows and Linux, and a browser extension for Chrome and Firefox. In short: everything it keeps stays on your computer, unless you choose to sign in to a Poro Tracker account (see below). No ads and no analytics.

What Poro Tracker keeps

  • Your decks and the deck you're playing.
  • Your match history: results, scores, dates, the format, your and your opponents' in-game names and legends, both decklists (unless a player made theirs private), battlefields, who went first, and which of your cards were drawn and played.
  • Notes you write on matches and replays.
  • Replays of your games, downloaded from Rift Atlas, and replays you import.
  • Your settings, and the card list (costs and types) read from Rift Atlas's page.

The desktop app keeps this in its data folder (or a folder you choose in Settings). The extension keeps it in your browser's storage for the extension. Neither sends it to us or to anyone else.

What it reads, and from where

  • Rift Atlas: while you play, Poro Tracker reads the game's live connection and what's on screen in the Rift Atlas page. After a game, it asks Rift Atlas's match history for both decklists, the official result and the replay, using your own Rift Atlas sign-in, the way Rift Atlas's own page does. These requests only read; your sign-in never leaves the Rift Atlas page. The only thing Poro Tracker ever clicks is Rift Atlas's "Private deck" box, when you turn that setting on.
  • Discord (desktop app, optional): when "Show my game on Discord" is on, the app tells the Discord app on your computer your legend, your opponent's legend, the score and how long the game has been going, to show on your Discord profile. Opponents' names are never shown. Turn it off in Settings.
  • App and extension sync: while both are running, they share your matches, decks, notes and replays directly on your computer (through 127.0.0.1). This never goes over the internet.
  • Updates: the desktop app checks GitHub for new versions and downloads them from there, and this website shows the latest version from GitHub. Like any download, GitHub sees your IP address; see GitHub's privacy statement.

If you sign in to an account (optional)

You can sign in with Discord in Settings → Account, so your history follows you to other computers and the app and the extension stay in step. Only if you do:

  • From Discord: your Discord user ID, display name, picture and email address, which Discord shares when you sign in. They're used to know it's you; your email isn't used for anything else.
  • Saved to your account: your matches, decks and replay notes (what's listed above, without your settings), and the replays you wrote notes on. Replays without notes stay on your computer.
  • Where: with Supabase, the cloud service that runs Poro Tracker's accounts. Other players can't see or change what's in your account, except what you share with a group you join (below). Poro Tracker's maintainer can access it, only to keep the service running and to fix problems you report. It's never sold.
  • Deleting it: Settings → Account → Delete my account and data removes your account and everything saved in it, right away, and takes you out of your groups with what you shared and wrote there. What's on your computer stays. Signing out keeps your account, so you can sign in again later.

Groups (optional)

With an account, you can start or join groups in the Hub, with an invite code from someone in the group or an invitation from a friend (see Friends). Groups are private: only their members see what's in them. If you're in a group:

  • Your matches: from the day you join, the group sees each match you play: the date, format, result and scores, both legends, your deck's name, your opponent's in-game name, battlefields, who went first, both decklists (unless a player made theirs private), and the replay. Never your own notes, and never which of your cards were drawn and played or your opening hands.
  • About you: your Discord display name and picture, your role in the group, when you joined, and whether you're sharing.
  • Live now: while "Show when I'm playing" is on, the group sees when you're in a game, both legends, the format, the game number and the score. Not your opponent's name. It's gone a few minutes after you stop playing.
  • What you add: notes you write in the group (on a replay or a matchup) and decks you post there. Everyone in the group can read them.
  • Your choices: in the Hub → Members, turn sharing off (the group stops seeing all your matches and replays right away), choose from which date matches are shared, keep decks out, or turn off Live now. In History, keep a single match out of a group. You can leave a group at any time.
  • What stays yours: leaving a group, being removed from one, or a group being deleted never deletes your matches. Copies of replays shared with groups are kept apart from your own and deleted once they're no longer shared. Deleting a group deletes its notes, posted decks and invites.

Friends (optional)

With an account, you have a friend code in the Friends section. Someone with your code, or someone in a group with you, can send you a friend request; you're friends once you accept. Nobody can look you up otherwise.

  • What friends see: your Discord display name and picture. Being friends shares no matches.
  • Invitations: a friend who owns or runs a group can invite you to it; you see the invitation in the Hub and join or decline. Joining is the same as joining with an invite code (see Groups).
  • Your choices: decline or cancel requests, remove a friend (groups you're both in don't change), or make a new friend code, which stops the old one working. Deleting your account deletes your friends, requests, code and invitations.

Sharing

Nothing is shared unless you do it: a group you join (see Groups), a backup file you export, or a replay you send with "Send with notes", which goes wherever you save or send it. Rift Atlas, Discord and GitHub handle what they receive under their own privacy policies.

Your choices

  • Settings can turn off saving match history and saving replays.
  • Delete any match, deck or replay from its tab; it's gone from the app and the extension.
  • Uninstalling the extension deletes everything it kept. For the app, delete its data folder (Settings → Your data → Open folder).

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version is posted here with a new date. Questions about your data, or anything else? Email contact@porotracker.com.
