You're invited to a group

Someone invited you to their group on Poro Tracker, the free tracker for Riftbound on Rift Atlas. In a group, everyone's matches come together: a shared matchup grid with the group's own playbook, the week's leaderboard, each other's replays to watch and add notes to, posted decks, and who's playing right now.

How to join

  1. Get Poro Tracker: the app for Windows or Linux, or the browser extension for Chrome or Firefox. Already have it? Make sure it's up to date.
  2. Open the Hub, the people icon in Poro Tracker's rail, and sign in with Discord.
  3. Join a group: paste the code, or this page's link, and press Join.

Codes work for 7 days. From the day you join, the group sees the matches you play: legends, deck names, results and scores, your opponents' names, decklists and replays, and never your own notes. You can share older matches, keep a deck or a match out, turn sharing off or leave at any time. More in the privacy policy.
